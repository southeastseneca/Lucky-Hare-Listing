About

Lucky Hare Brewing Company

Tucked along the hillside of Seneca Lake in Hector, NY, Lucky Hare Brewing Company is one of the Finger Lakes' most beloved craft taprooms. Perched less than 15 minutes north of Watkins Glen, this family- and dog-friendly destination draws locals and visitors alike with its relaxed atmosphere, sweeping lake views, and a rotating lineup of expertly crafted beers.

The Hector taproom pours 10 of Lucky Hare's own beers on any given day, alongside house-made cocktails, local Finger Lakes wines, cider, and non-alcoholic options — something for everyone in your crew. The back deck is the place to be in summer: open air, green lawn rolling toward the lake, and the kind of golden-hour sunsets you'll be talking about long after you leave.

Hungry? Babe's Burgers operates right inside the taproom with a dedicated food window on the back deck. Their menu goes well beyond burgers — with gluten-free and vegetarian options — and you can order ahead online. Lucky Hare has also become a hub for community events, from the annual Finger Lakes National Forest Cleanup to the beloved South East Seneca Cook-Off.

In Our Words

Our Story

There's a reason Lucky Hare shows up on nearly every "must-visit" list in the area — and it has everything to do with owners Ian and Denise. These two pour their heart and soul into every pint, and you feel it the moment you walk through the door. The beer is world-class, genuinely, but the atmosphere never takes itself too seriously. It's relaxed, it's fun, and that sprawling yard out back might be one of the finest places to spend a summer afternoon in all of the Finger Lakes.

If you haven't tried the Milk Stout yet, that's where you start. It's an all-time favorite for good reason — smooth, rich, and completely approachable even if stouts aren't usually your thing. Lucky Hare is the kind of place that earns regulars fast, and keeps them for life.

— South East Seneca

Plan Your Visit

Tips for Visitors

  • Open daily at noon — Sun through Tues until 8 PM, Wed through Sat until 9 PM.
  • Wednesday is "Friendsday" — live music 6–8 PM, a great mid-week outing.
  • Thursday is Trivia Night with Doc Robinson, 6–8 PM — come early to grab a table.
  • Friday and Saturday nights bring rotating live music all summer long — check Instagram for the weekly lineup.
  • Babe's Burgers has its own line (607-546-2010) — call ahead or order online on busy weekends.
  • Dogs and kids welcome on the outdoor patio and lawn. Bring a blanket for warm evenings.
  • Gift cards available but cannot be used for Babe's Burgers — they're a separate business.
  • Combine with the Finger Lakes Wine Trail — acclaimed wineries within 10 min along Route 414.
  • Follow @luckyharebrewing on Instagram for the latest tap list and event announcements.
